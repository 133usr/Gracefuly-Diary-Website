Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Graceful Diary (Android package thank.you.God.Elohim, referred to as "we", "our", or "the App") is an offline-first gratitude journal application designed around local storage and user control. We do not operate developer-owned backend servers, user accounts, or central databases to store your journal content.

This Privacy Policy describes what information Graceful Diary accesses, processes locally on your device, transmits to third-party services, collects for diagnostics, and shares—based on the actual implementation of the App.

3. Data Processed Locally on Your Device

  • Journal Content & Features: Daily gratitude entries, full-text search indexes (FTS4), Time Capsules (titles, prompts, letters, replies, unlock dates, recipient labels), and Garden/Sanctuary milestones are stored in a local SQLite database (Entry_database) inside the App's private sandbox.
  • Photo Memories & Permissions: When you attach photos to a journal entry or Time Capsule (up to 3 photos per item), Graceful Diary uses the native Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). The App does not request broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES). Selected images are decoded, scaled (up to 1600px), and re-encoded as WebP files inside app-private storage (files/memories/), which removes original camera EXIF metadata (such as GPS coordinates).
  • Optional Profile Name & Settings: If you enter a display/diary name, or configure themes, fonts, reminder schedules, language, or app-lock preferences, those values are stored locally in Android SharedPreferences.
  • Biometric App Lock: Optional biometric locking for the App or Time Capsules uses Android's native BiometricPrompt API. The App only receives a success or failure callback and never accesses, stores, or transmits biometric data.
  • Local Exports & Share Cards: You can export entries to CSV, JSON, or PDF (including embedded photo thumbnails) to a location you choose via Android's Storage Access Framework. When you share a quote card, a temporary PNG is written to the App's cache directory and shared via SharingFileProvider solely with the target app you select.
  • Device Alarms & Notifications: Daily reminders and weekly digests are scheduled locally using Android AlarmManager and broadcast receivers (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE). Notification content is generated on your device.

4. Google Sign-In & Google Drive Sync (Optional)

Cloud backup and sync are completely optional. If you choose to connect your Google Account in Settings, the App uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive REST API v3 as follows:

  • Authentication & Identity: The App requests your basic Google Account email address (requestEmail()) to authenticate your session and display the connected account in Settings.
  • Restricted Scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): The App requests access only to Google Drive's hidden, app-specific Application Data Folder (appDataFolder). The App cannot view, read, modify, or delete your personal Drive files, documents, or Google Photos outside this hidden folder.
  • What Is Backed Up: When sync runs (manually, on change, or periodically via Android WorkManager), the App uploads a structured JSON file (graceful_diary_sync.json) containing your journal entries, Time Capsules, photo metadata, deletion tombstones, Sanctuary/Garden state, optional display name, and app settings, along with your compressed WebP photo memory files (image/webp), directly to your personal appDataFolder.
  • Transport & Storage Custody: All communication with Google Drive occurs over HTTPS/TLS directly between your device and Google's servers. The developer has no access to your Google Drive files. Note that the JSON and WebP backup files are stored in your private appDataFolder in structured form and are not separately end-to-end encrypted by the App prior to upload.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Graceful Diary's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Google user data is used solely to provide user-facing backup, synchronization, and restoration of your journal data within your own Google Drive appDataFolder.
  • We do not transfer, sell, or disclose Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or marketing.
  • We do not use Google user data to train, fine-tune, or improve generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • Humans do not read your Google Drive backup data; it resides solely in your personal Google account.

6. Analytics, Crash Reporting & Other Network Requests

  • Firebase Analytics & Firebase Crashlytics: To understand app stability and feature usage, Graceful Diary integrates Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
    • What is collected: Screen views, non-content interaction events (e.g., opening settings, creating an entry, milestone level-ups, biometric prompt outcomes), app instance identifiers, crash stack traces, and non-fatal exception logs (including handled Drive sync exceptions). No journal entry text, Time Capsule content, or photo memories are intentionally logged to Analytics or Crashlytics.
    • Advertising ID Disabled: The App explicitly disables Google Analytics Advertising ID collection in its manifest (google_analytics_adid_collection_enabled = false) and displays no ads.
    • Opt-Out Control: Analytics and Crashlytics are enabled by default, and you can opt out at any time in Settings. Opting out disables both Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection and calls resetAnalyticsData() to clear the local app instance ID.
  • Android Auto Backup: The App permits standard Android OS backup (android:allowBackup="true"), excluding the local sync receipt file (google_sync_device_receipt.xml). If you enable device backup in your Android system settings, Android may back up your local app database and preferences to your Google account according to Google's Android Backup Service terms.
  • Google Play In-App Updates: The App uses the Google Play App Update library to check for and prompt you to install newer versions from Google Play.
  • Optional Support & Feedback Emails: If you choose to send private feedback from the in-app review prompt, the App opens your email client (mailto:) with a draft addressed to liebemutter07@gmail.com containing your rating, message, app version, device model, and Android OS version. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app.

7. Data Retention, Deletion & Your Controls

You control your data across both your device and your Google Drive:

  • Delete Individual or Selected Entries & Photos: Use the in-app Manage entries & photos screen (or entry editor) to delete specific entries or photos. Local files and database rows are deleted immediately; if Google Drive Sync is connected, matching Drive photo files are deleted and deletion tombstones are synced to graceful_diary_sync.json (or queued until you are next online).
  • Disconnect Google Account: You can sign out of Google Drive Sync at any time in Settings. This stops background WorkManager sync jobs and clears your local Google account preference.
  • Delete All Cloud Backups: To permanently remove the hidden appDataFolder backup from your Google Drive, go to Google Drive on the web → Settings (gear icon) → Manage apps → Graceful Diary → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke Google Account Permissions: You can revoke Graceful Diary's OAuth access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.
  • Remove All Local Data: Uninstalling Graceful Diary or clearing App Storage in Android System Settings permanently deletes the local Room database, photo memories, and preferences from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

Graceful Diary is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App or via support email, please contact us so we can address it promptly.
