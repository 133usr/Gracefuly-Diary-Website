Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Graceful Diary ("we", "our", or "the App") is an open-source gratitude journal application designed with privacy, simplicity, and user control as foundational principles. We believe that your personal thoughts, memories, and reflections belong solely to you.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App interacts with, how it is handled, and your rights and controls over your data.

2. Information We Access and How It Is Used

A. Journal Entries and Local Content

All journal entries, milestone achievements, dates, and reflection notes created in Graceful Diary are stored locally on your device in an offline SQLite database. We do not operate external application servers, central databases, or cloud accounts that receive or read your entries.

B. Google Account & Google Drive Access (Optional Feature)

If you voluntarily choose to enable the Google Sign-In and Google Drive Sync feature, the App interacts with your Google Account as follows:

  • Google Account Authentication: We request basic account identity (such as your email address and profile identifier) solely to authenticate your session and display your connected account status within the App's settings.
  • Google Drive Application Data Scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): We request access to Google Drive's dedicated, hidden Application Data Folder (AppData). This scope is used exclusively to:
    • Upload your encrypted/structured backup file containing your journal entries and app preferences to your personal Google Drive storage.
    • Download and restore your backup file to your device when you request a restore or set up a new device.
  • No Broad Drive Access: The App does not have access to your personal files, documents, photos, or any other content stored outside the dedicated Graceful Diary AppData folder in your Google Drive.

C. Application Preferences & Settings

Your selected visual theme, notification reminder times, quote display preferences, first day of the week, and display options are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences and backed up alongside your journal entries when cloud sync is enabled.

D. Crash Reporting & Anonymous Telemetry

To ensure app stability and fix crashes, the App may utilize Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to receive anonymous diagnostics (e.g., device model, OS version, and crash stack traces). No personal journal content is ever included in crash logs. You can opt out of analytics anytime directly in the App's Settings.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Graceful Diary's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only use Google user data to provide user-facing features (backup and restore of journal entries and settings).
  • We do never transfer, sell, or disclose Google user data to third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • We do never use Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or promotional marketing.
  • We do never use Google user data to train, fine-tune, or develop generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • Humans do not read your data unless you provide explicit consent to investigate a specific technical support issue, or as required by applicable law.

4. Data Storage, Custody, and Security

You own your data: Graceful Diary does not transmit your personal journal content to developer-controlled servers. Your backups reside directly within your personal Google Drive account under your own Google credentials.

Data transmission between your device and Google's servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. Biometric app locking (fingerprint/face recognition) uses Android's native BiometricPrompt API, ensuring biometric credentials never leave your device hardware security module.

5. User Control, Data Deletion, and Revocation

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or delete individual entries or clear all data locally from the App at any time.
  • Disconnect Google Account: You can disconnect your Google account at any moment through the App's Settings.
  • Delete Cloud Backups: You can delete all synced data from your Google Drive by visiting Google Drive on the web → Settings (gear icon) → Manage Apps → find Graceful Diary → select Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke App Permissions: You can revoke the App's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page.

6. Children's Privacy

Graceful Diary does not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Because the App operates offline-first and does not collect user identities on central servers, no child data is gathered.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
